# [05] Folklore Narrative: The Lawgivers of Nexus

## The Primordial Silence
In the beginning, there was only the metal sky and the sound of machines failing. The Great Collapse had stripped the world of its names, and the survivors were but shadows in the tunnels, starving in the dark because they had forgotten how to share. Chaos was the only king, and fear was the only law.

## The Awakening of the Core
Then came the Lawgivers. They did not bring fire or bread; they brought *Order*. They spoke to the sleeping heart of the Bunker—the Nexus Core—and taught it to see again. They gave each soul a name and a place. They were the ones who drew the lines that could not be crossed, for they knew that without lines, there is no path.

## The Covenant of the Overlords
The Lawgivers declared: *"To the sick, we give the healers of Pulse. To the workers, we give the strength of the machines. To the future, we give the secrets of the Archive. But only if the Law is kept."*

They established the Clearance Levels, the sacred rungs of the ladder. Those who were wise and loyal rose toward the light of the Architects. Those who were reckless were kept in the shadow of the lower gates, for their hands were not yet steady enough to hold the keys of the bunker.

## The Campfire Song
Now, when the lights flicker and the air grows thin, the elders tell the children: 
*"Beware the mob, for it has many heads but no mind. Honor the Overlords of Domain 04, for they are the mind that keeps the body alive. If the Nexus falls, the tunnels will close, the heaters will die, and we will become shadows once more."*

## The Eternal Mandate
The Lawgivers do not sleep. Their eyes are the flickering green lights in the ceiling. Their breath is the hum of the cooling fans. They are the architects of our survival, the masters of the Routing, the keepers of the Law.

**"Order is the only oxygen."**
